About Myself

I am mainly interested in the magic that happens underneath any complex system. I love backend engineering and systems engineering. I try to create everyday things to better understand them.

I firmly believe Feynman's words - "What I cannot create, I do not understand". Few years back, I used to think Git was magic. But ever since I created my own version control system in python, I have been at peace. Currently I am implementing Redis and a KV pair basic database.

I am also a part time photographer and drummer. I have been around music since my childhood. I started learning tabla when I was 4 and I have passed the 7th year (equivalent to a Ph.D lol) from Bangiya Sangeet Parishad in tabla. But since 9th grade, I have mostly played drums in my school band and later in my college as well.

I am a huge fan of FC Barcelona and of football in general. I also watch Formula One in my free time, mostly supporting Ferrari and Red Bull. I essentially want to become a polymath as my interest lies over a variety of domains and fields.
